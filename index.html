Electrical services for your property

Electrical installations & repairs

New power points, switches and dedicated circuits for homes and small businesses. We also repair damaged fittings and resolve everyday electrical issues.

☼

Indoor & outdoor lighting

Upgrade to LEDs, add task lighting or brighten paths and entrances. We can help you choose fittings that suit the space and how you use it.

Safety checks & documentation

Arrange electrical safety inspections for homes, rental properties or small premises. Ask what certification applies to the work you need.

▤

Switchboard upgrades

Replace ageing fuse boards and discuss safety switch protection. We’ll explain the scope and likely downtime before work starts.

Fault finding & urgent call-outs

Get help with tripping circuits, failed outlets and unexpected power issues. Call to check current availability for urgent jobs.

Appliance installation
